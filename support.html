Common questions

Do I need a Garmin Connect account?

No. Formcast works fully on Apple Health data alone — skip the data-source step during setup and grant Health access instead. Connecting Garmin (or Fitbit) just adds extra metrics from your watch that Apple Health alone doesn't have.

Can I use Fitbit instead of Garmin?

Yes — pick Fitbit during setup (or in Settings). Fitbit's own developer API is being retired, so this signs in through the new Google Health API instead: you'll see Google's own sign-in screen, not Fitbit's. Today this syncs your workouts, steps, and sleep; resting heart rate and heart-rate variability aren't available through Google's new API yet. You can only have one data source connected at a time — sign out of Garmin first if you want to switch to Fitbit, or vice versa.

Do I need to pay for anything?

Formcast itself is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. The AI coaching features need either Apple's free on-device model (available on newer iPhones) or your own API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google — those providers bill you directly for what you use; Formcast never sees or marks up that cost.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete everything. This signs you out of Garmin, removes any saved API key, and erases every synced activity, health record and plan from your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My Garmin sign-in isn't working

Garmin has no official third-party API, so Formcast signs in the same way the Garmin Connect app does — email, password, and a 2-factor code if you have one enabled. If it's failing, double-check your password works in the real Garmin Connect app first, then email support with what step fails.

My Fitbit/Google sign-in isn't working

This app is still in Google's "testing" status while it's new, which means only Google accounts the developer has explicitly added as test users can sign in — everyone else sees an "app not verified" screen. If that's you, email support with the Google account email you'd like added.

How does the PT tab decide what I should do next week?

The "Next week" card combines three things, all calculated on your device: your training load (if it's running high, it suggests an easier week with roughly a third less volume instead of pushing on), your aim from the PT intake (Get better pushes when you're recovered and plans an easier week after three rising weeks, Stay fit keeps things steady and checks the basics of 150+ minutes of cardio and two strength days a week, Lose weight builds steady volume plus strength work), and what you actually did: for lifts it compares your last weight and reps and suggests more reps or the next weight; for running, riding and swimming it suggests a small step up from your longest session. If you follow a plan in the app, the card lists that plan's sessions for next week and adjusts them for your load instead. You can change your aim any time from the button at the top right of the PT tab.

Why does my PT tell me to take it easy?

Your recent training load is higher than your body has been used to, which raises injury and burnout risk. A lighter week, with a little pace kept in, lets your fitness settle. If you'd rather not get this advice, set your aim to Stay fit in the PT intake, though an overloaded week will still be flagged.

How much does the AI coach cost me, and can I cap it?

Settings → AI usage shows this month's calls, tokens and an approximate cost, using your provider's own token counts when it reports them (otherwise a size-based estimate; the cost uses rough list prices and can differ from your bill). Under Daily call limit you can cap how many AI calls Formcast makes per day; once reached, the coach waits until tomorrow. Max output per call caps how long each AI answer can be (2,500 tokens is recommended; normal answers are 100–600), and a request whose input would exceed about 15,000 tokens is refused before it's sent. Tap the ⓘ next to the section title for a plain-language explanation of calls and tokens. A normal day is about three calls (daily briefing, weekly review, notifications); a 12-week plan takes about 13. The on-device Apple model is free and isn't counted. For a hard spending cap, also set a monthly limit in your provider's dashboard.

How do I load my older Garmin history?

Settings → Sync → Load full history and pick 1, 2, 5 years or everything (up to 10). It reads one day at a time, so keep the app open — the screen stays awake while it runs. If it stops, run it again: days that are already stored are skipped, so it continues where it left off. "Sync now" only goes back as far as the First sync setting (30–365 days).

How do I export my data?

Settings → Your data → Export data. Tick what you want (activities, daily metrics, sleep, HRV, strength sets, planned workouts, coach messages), choose CSV or JSON and a period, then tap Export and pick where to send the file. It contains what Formcast has synced to your phone, in metric units. GPS tracks and second-by-second data aren't stored in the app; for everything Garmin has ever collected about you, use the data export in your Garmin account settings.

Is my data sent to a Formcast server?

No — there is no Formcast server. Everything stays on your device except the compact summary (including the aim and notes you entered in the PT intake) sent directly from your phone to whichever AI provider you've chosen, to generate your coaching text.
