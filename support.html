Common questions

Do I need a Garmin Connect account?

No. With an Apple Watch (or just an iPhone) pick Apple Watch / Apple Health as your data source during setup, or later under Settings → Data source, and allow access in the screen that follows. Formcast then reads your workouts (with route and heart rate), steps, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep with stages, active energy and VO₂ max straight from the Health app on your iPhone — no account, nothing leaves your phone. A workout's route and heart-rate chart are loaded when you open it. Garmin and Fitbit give a few extra metrics Apple Health doesn't have (such as Body Battery and stress); you use one source at a time. If nothing shows up, open the iPhone Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Formcast and turn all categories on, and make sure your Apple Watch is syncing. Workouts Formcast recorded itself aren't imported a second time.

Can I use Fitbit instead of Garmin?

Yes — pick Fitbit during setup (or in Settings). Fitbit's own developer API is being retired, so this signs in through the new Google Health API instead: you'll see Google's own sign-in screen, not Fitbit's. Today this syncs your workouts, steps, and sleep; resting heart rate and heart-rate variability aren't available through Google's new API yet. You can only have one data source connected at a time — sign out of Garmin first if you want to switch to Fitbit, or vice versa.

Do I need to pay for anything?

Formcast itself is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. The AI coaching features need either Apple's free on-device model (available on newer iPhones) or your own API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google — those providers bill you directly for what you use; Formcast never sees or marks up that cost. An API key is not the same as a ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini subscription: the API is a separate pay-per-use account on the provider's developer site (platform.openai.com, console.anthropic.com or aistudio.google.com), where you add a payment method or credit first — $5 is plenty to start, and the coach costs a few cents a day. Without credit every request fails; the app tells you so.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete everything. This signs you out of Garmin, removes any saved API key, and erases every synced activity, health record and plan from your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My Garmin sign-in isn't working

Garmin has no official third-party API, so Formcast signs in the same way the Garmin Connect app does — email, password, and a 2-factor code if you have one enabled. If it's failing, double-check your password works in the real Garmin Connect app first, then email support with what step fails.

My Fitbit/Google sign-in isn't working

This app is still in Google's "testing" status while it's new, which means only Google accounts the developer has explicitly added as test users can sign in — everyone else sees an "app not verified" screen. If that's you, email support with the Google account email you'd like added.

How do gym workouts work (routines, supersets, rest timer)?

Activities → + → Gym workout. Under My routines tap New routine to prepare a workout at home: pick exercises, then set the number of sets, a rep range and the rest time for each, and link two exercises as a superset if you do them back to back. Tap a routine in the gym to start it: every set is pre-filled with what you did last time (shown in the Previous column). Adjust kg and reps if needed and tick the set when it's done — the rest timer starts (no rest between the exercises of a superset) and can ping you with a notification when the rest is over. Tap an exercise name for a demonstration picture. If you leave the app mid-workout you can resume it from the Gym screen. Not in the mood to write down numbers? Use ⋯ → Done without numbers on an exercise, or choose Count them as done when you finish: the exercise is saved without sets and counts as trained, and the coach won't say you skipped it.

Can I track my body weight?

Yes: Activities → + → Gym workout → Body weight. Add weigh-ins now and then to see the trend and your weekly change, and say whether you're bulking, cutting or maintaining so the coach reads a rising or falling weight correctly. Weigh-ins stay on your device; the recent ones are part of the short summary the AI coach reads.

How many sessions will the PT suggest for next week?

As many as you say you want: in the PT intake choose how often you want to train per week (2× to 7×). The Suggested week on the PT tab then fills exactly that many sessions, spread over the sports you actually do and the ones you marked as focus sports, with an easy/hard mix that follows your training load. The per-sport details (weights, paces) are folded away under Details per sport.

Where is my data backed up, and how do I restore it?

Formcast writes a backup file of everything you created yourself (routines, weigh-ins, workouts you logged or recorded, journal, plans and goals) after each gym workout and weigh-in, and at most once a day when you open the app. The last seven are kept in the Files app under On My iPhone → Formcast → Backups, and they are part of your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup. To restore, go to Settings → Back-up → Restore from a back-up… and pick a file: only what is missing is added, nothing is overwritten. Data synced from Garmin, Fitbit or Apple Health isn't in the file — it comes back by syncing.

What are records, estimated 1RM and the muscle-group bars?

Gym → Strength progress shows, for this week and last, how many sets you did per muscle group, and every lift you logged with weights. Tap a lift for a chart of your estimated one-rep max (Epley formula, only used for sets of 1–12 reps), your top weight and volume, and your records. After a gym workout you see any new records. Exercises you marked done without numbers count as trained, but add nothing to volume or records.

What is the journal ("How was yesterday?")?

A card on the Dashboard where you tap what happened yesterday that your watch can't see: caffeine, alcohol, a late meal, a stressful day, illness, travel, pain, supplements, or something of your own. After enough days the PT tab shows patterns — for example "on days with alcohol, your next-morning HRV was 12% lower" — but only when there are at least 5 days with and 5 days without it and a clear difference. These are patterns in your own data, not proof of a cause, and days you didn't fill in are never counted as "nothing happened".

Lock screen timer and the Today widget

While a gym workout runs, the lock screen and the Dynamic Island show your routine, set count and a rest countdown that keeps running without opening the app (allow Live Activities when iOS asks). Long-press the home screen → + → Formcast to add the Today widget with your readiness, the coach's line and today's session; open Formcast once after a sync so it has numbers.

How does the PT tab decide what I should do next week?

The "Next week" card combines three things, all calculated on your device: your training load (if it's running high, it suggests an easier week with roughly a third less volume instead of pushing on), your aim from the PT intake (Get better pushes when you're recovered and plans an easier week after three rising weeks, Stay fit keeps things steady and checks the basics of 150+ minutes of cardio and two strength days a week, Lose weight builds steady volume plus strength work), and what you actually did: for lifts it compares your last weight and reps and suggests more reps or the next weight; for running, riding and swimming it suggests a small step up from your longest session. If you follow a plan in the app, the card lists that plan's sessions for next week and adjusts them for your load instead. You can set a different aim per focus sport — for example Get better for running but Stay fit for the gym — in the PT intake under Aim per sport. You can change your aims any time from the button at the top right of the PT tab.

Why does my PT tell me to take it easy?

Your recent training load is higher than your body has been used to, which raises injury and burnout risk. A lighter week, with a little pace kept in, lets your fitness settle. If you'd rather not get this advice, set your aim to Stay fit in the PT intake, though an overloaded week will still be flagged.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes. It shows your readiness score, today's coach line and planned workout, and this week's totals, and offers watch-face complications (circular, rectangular, corner and inline). It receives everything from the Formcast app on your iPhone, so open Formcast on the phone once and give it a moment to send. Install it from the Watch app on your iPhone (My Watch → Available Apps → Formcast) or let "Show App on Apple Watch" do it automatically; for TestFlight builds it appears there too. It can also record workouts: choose Run, Walk, Ride, Hike or Strength on the Watch (page four), or on the iPhone tap + in Activities → Record with Apple Watch and the Watch app opens and starts. The Watch measures heart rate, energy and — for outdoor sports — distance and a GPS route, and saves the workout to Apple Health; while it runs, the iPhone app shows it live (time, heart rate, distance, pace, energy and the route) — pause or stop it on the Watch; the workout appears in Formcast after the next sync (and for Garmin or Fitbit users too, because those are the workouts you recorded with Formcast).

Which AI model does Formcast use, and what if it says a model isn't available?

You don't have to choose one. By default Formcast asks your provider which models your key can use and picks the best fit (Claude Sonnet, GPT mini or Gemini Flash), then adopts newer ones by itself. If your account refuses a model, Formcast tries the next best one automatically. If every model fails, it nearly always means the provider account has no payment method or credit yet — an API key is billed separately from a ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini subscription — so add one at the provider's developer site and try again. You can still pick a specific model under Settings → Coach engine → Model; the list only shows models your account can use.

How much does the AI coach cost me, and can I cap it?

Settings → AI usage shows this month's calls, tokens and an approximate cost, using your provider's own token counts when it reports them (otherwise a size-based estimate; the cost uses rough list prices and can differ from your bill). Under Daily call limit you can cap how many AI calls Formcast makes per day (25, 50 — recommended —, 100 or 200); once reached, the coach waits until tomorrow. Calls your provider refuses (wrong key, no credit) aren't counted. Max output per call caps how long each AI answer can be (2,500 tokens is recommended; normal answers are 100–600), and a request whose input would exceed about 15,000 tokens is refused before it's sent. Tap the ⓘ next to the section title for a plain-language explanation of calls and tokens. A normal day is about three calls (daily briefing, weekly review, notifications) plus one per question, chart insight or workout read — the first day, while you look around, can be 10 to 20; a 12-week plan takes about 13. The on-device Apple model is free and isn't counted. For a hard spending cap, also set a monthly limit in your provider's dashboard.

How do I load my older Garmin history?

Settings → Sync → Load full history and pick 1, 2, 5 years or everything (up to 10). It reads one day at a time, so keep the app open — the screen stays awake while it runs. If it stops, run it again: days that are already stored are skipped, so it continues where it left off. "Sync now" only goes back as far as the First sync setting (30–365 days).

How do I export my data?

Settings → Your data → Export data. Tick what you want (activities, daily metrics, sleep, HRV, strength sets, planned workouts, coach messages), choose CSV or JSON and a period, then tap Export and pick where to send the file. It contains what Formcast has synced to your phone, in metric units. GPS tracks and second-by-second data aren't stored in the app, so for those use Detailed, straight from Garmin further down the same screen (Garmin accounts): pick a period (for example the last year) and the datasets you want — sleep with stages, HRV with its 5-minute readings, heart rate, stress and Body Battery through the day, respiration, SpO₂, weight, training readiness and more, and per activity the details, GPS track (GPX), TCX or the original Garmin FIT file. You get one zip with Garmin's raw data as JSON lines, a CSV with one row per day for every field and a CSV per time series, plus a manifest listing what had data. It reads from Garmin one day at a time (about 10 minutes for three datasets over a year), so keep the app open; try a short period first. Not every watch records every dataset.

Is my data sent to a Formcast server?

No — there is no Formcast server. Everything stays on your device except the compact summary (including the aim and notes you entered in the PT intake) sent directly from your phone to whichever AI provider you've chosen, to generate your coaching text.
