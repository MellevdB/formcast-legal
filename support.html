Common questions

Do I need a Garmin Connect account?

No. Formcast works fully on Apple Health data alone — skip the Garmin sign-in step during setup and grant Health access instead. Connecting Garmin just adds a few extra metrics (Body Battery, stress, richer activity detail) that only Garmin devices report.

Do I need to pay for anything?

Formcast itself is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. The AI coaching features need either Apple's free on-device model (available on newer iPhones) or your own API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google — those providers bill you directly for what you use; Formcast never sees or marks up that cost.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete everything. This signs you out of Garmin, removes any saved API key, and erases every synced activity, health record and plan from your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My Garmin sign-in isn't working

Garmin has no official third-party API, so Formcast signs in the same way the Garmin Connect app does — email, password, and a 2-factor code if you have one enabled. If it's failing, double-check your password works in the real Garmin Connect app first, then email support with what step fails.

Is my data sent to a Formcast server?

No — there is no Formcast server. Everything stays on your device except the compact summary sent directly from your phone to whichever AI provider you've chosen, to generate your coaching text.
